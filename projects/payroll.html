Payroll

One screen, one week, one shot to win a client pitch — designed against references like Google Pay and Cred.

Role

UX designer, working under a senior designer/mentor

Timeline

1 week

Tools

  • Figma
  • Axure
  • Illustrator
  • Zeplin

Brief

The client wanted a single home-screen UI for a payments app, using Google Pay and Cred as references, to gauge whether the team could deliver quality UI work and match the client's vision — with the goal of winning a longer engagement.

What changed after feedback

The first version drew criticism from my mentor for looking too close to Google Pay — a copy rather than a fresh solution. That triggered a full restart:

  • Redesigned from scratch to be distinct while staying familiar to users of similar apps
  • Introduced a style guide after the first version lacked one, fixing inconsistent spacing and theming across sections
  • Adopted the iOS style guide for the final versions, ensuring consistency across iOS and Android

Final direction

A dark, corporate visual style — simple, elegant, and professional, designed for both iOS and Android from a shared iOS-guideline base. The final design was approved by both the mentor and the client lead.

Learnings

The core lesson was about originality under reference-driven constraints — it's easy to let a strong reference (Google Pay, Cred) collapse into imitation. Getting direct, blunt feedback early and being willing to restart from zero turned out to matter more than protecting the first draft.
