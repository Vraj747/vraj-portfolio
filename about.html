About

Software developer & UX designer, building things people can actually use.

I'm a final-term student in IT Innovation & Design at Conestoga College, based in Ontario, Canada. I move between code and design deliberately — I want the things I build to be technically sound and genuinely usable, not just one or the other.

Background

I'm self-taught in design, and formally trained in software development — that combination shapes how I work. I like translating fuzzy problems into concrete solutions, whether that means running user interviews or writing the code that ships. I've led design and development teams, mentored newer designers, and worked directly with stakeholders to turn ambiguous requirements into working products.

Outside of work, I play football and volleyball, play guitar and piano, sing, and do drama skits — a mix that keeps me comfortable performing, collaborating, and improvising under pressure, all of which shows up in client-facing design work.
